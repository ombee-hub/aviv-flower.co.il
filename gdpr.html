Transfers outside the EU

Aviv is based in Israel. The European Commission has recognised Israel as providing an adequate level of data protection (Decision 2011/61/EU), so personal data can be transferred to us without additional safeguards. Where data goes to any other country outside the EEA, we use appropriate safeguards such as the Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15).
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16).
  • Erasure — have your data deleted where there's no longer a reason to keep it (Art. 17).
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data (Art. 18).
  • Portability — receive data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format (Art. 20).
  • Objection — object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21).
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

We don't make decisions about you by automated means alone, don't profile visitors, and don't send marketing without your consent.

How to make a request

Email yifat@aviv-flower.co.il or write to Aviv Flower Ltd, Gesher Ha'etz Street 27, PO Box 12007, Ind. Park Emek Hefer 3877701, Israel. We'll reply within one month, free of charge. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request.

Complaints

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work, or where the issue happened — for example, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the data protection authority of your German federal state. In Israel, the supervisory authority is the Privacy Protection Authority.
