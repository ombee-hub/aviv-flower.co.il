Privacy policy

How Aviv Flower Ltd handles personal information on this website — what we collect, why, how long we keep it and the choices you have.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

This website, www.aviv-flower.co.il, is operated by Aviv Flower Ltd ("Aviv", "we"), Gesher Ha'etz Street 27, PO Box 12007, Ind. Park Emek Hefer 3877701, Israel. Tel. +972-4-6126000. Our subsidiaries Aviv Holland (Netherlands) and Aviv GmbH (Germany) are part of the same group.

What we collect

  • When you contact us. If you use the contact form, email or call us, we receive the details you give us — typically your name, email address, company, the topic and your message.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our web server and hosting provider automatically record technical details of each visit: IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time. We use them to keep the site secure and working, not to identify you.
  • What we don't do. We don't run analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website, and we don't sell personal information.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your enquiry, prepare quotes and orders, manage our business relationship with you, keep the website secure, and meet our legal obligations.

Cookies and local storage

This website doesn't set its own cookies. It keeps three small preferences in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to us:

NameWhat it remembers
aviv-langThe language you chose (English or Chinese)
aviv-a11yYour accessibility settings, such as bigger text or high contrast
aviv-cookie-noticeThat you answered the cookie notice, so it isn't shown again

Our hosting and security provider may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from attacks. You can clear all of these at any time in your browser settings.

Who we share it with

  • Within the Aviv group (Israel, the Netherlands and Germany), when it's needed to handle your enquiry or order.
  • Service providers that run the website, email and form delivery for us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Other websites

The WebShop (aviv.newstory.cloud) and the growers portal (login.aviv-flower.co.il) are separate services with their own terms and privacy notices, shown when you register. This policy doesn't cover other websites we link to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to handle the enquiry and the business relationship that follows, or as long as the law requires — for example, for accounting records. Server logs are kept for a short period.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information.

Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, you may ask to see the information we hold about you and to correct it. Visitors from the European Union, the EEA and the United Kingdom have further rights under the GDPR — see our GDPR information. To make a request, write to yifat@aviv-flower.co.il.

Children

This website is meant for business customers and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: yifat@aviv-flower.co.il, or by post to Aviv Flower Ltd, Gesher Ha'etz Street 27, PO Box 12007, Ind. Park Emek Hefer 3877701, Israel. Tel. +972-4-6126000.
