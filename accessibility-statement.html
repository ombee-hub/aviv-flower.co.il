Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Aviv Flower is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. This page describes what we provide and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

What this site provides

An accessibility panel is available on every page (the round green button at the bottom-left corner). It offers larger text, high-contrast and dark modes, highlighted links, increased text and line spacing, a readability font, paused animations, hidden images, an enlarged cursor, grayscale mode and a reading guide. Choices are remembered between visits.

Beyond the panel, the site is built with semantic HTML, full keyboard navigation with visible focus states, alternative text on images, and respect for the reduced-motion system preference.

Conformance target

We aim to conform to WCAG 2.1 level AA and the Israeli standard IS 5568. Some third-party services linked from this site (the WebShop and the growers portal) are operated separately and may differ.

Found a problem?

If any part of this site is difficult for you to use, please tell us and we will fix it: contact page or email flowers@aviv-flower.co.il.
